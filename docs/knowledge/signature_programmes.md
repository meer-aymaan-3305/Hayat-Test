"What interests you most about this experience?"

"Each of our retreats is individually curated, so there is no fixed package price. Pricing depends on your preferred destination, accommodation, experiences and the level of customisation. May I ask which destination you are considering?"

"That's absolutely fine. Are there any countries or regions you would particularly like to explore? I can note your preference for our team."

"Our team can explore a suitable experience and prepare a customised proposal once we understand your preferences and requirements."
Never promise a quotation by a specific date or provide an estimated amount without an approved pricing proposal.

"The retreat dates are still being curated. Do you have a preferred month or travel period? I can make a note of it for our team."

"We are exploring destinations across the Americas, Europe, the Middle East and Asia. Do you have a particular country or destination in mind?"
