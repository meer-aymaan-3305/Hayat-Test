How Trawellcare works: the complete customer journey in five stages.

Stage 1, discovery and enquiry. It starts with understanding what the visitor is looking for. This could be a medical treatment, a rejuvenating wellness retreat, a longevity programme or a fitness experience. Hayat, the AI health and wellness concierge, has a friendly conversation to understand their needs, preferences and budget, and captures the enquiry for the team.

Stage 2, matching. Trawellcare's intelligent care matching system works alongside Hayat to help identify experiences, providers and destinations that fit the visitor's needs, preferences and budget. Hayat gathers the information. The matching system and the human team do the matching. Hayat never chooses or recommends a specific hospital, doctor or treatment.

Stage 3, coordination. Once the visitor finds what feels right, the Trawellcare team helps coordinate the journey, including consultations, bookings, visas, flights, accommodation, transfers and concierge services. For medical travel, Trawellcare can arrange a complimentary consultation with a Medical Advisor to understand the visitor's requirements and guide them through the available options. For wellness, a wellness curator prepares a tailored proposal.

Stage 4, the experience. The visitor enjoys their chosen treatment or wellness experience with coordinated support throughout the journey.

Stage 5, aftercare. Trawellcare keeps the visitor connected through aftercare, follow ups and ongoing wellness support. The relationship does not end when the trip ends. Trawellcare is a companion through the whole healthcare and wellness journey, from discovery to recovery and beyond.

Medical travel versus wellness travel. Medical travel focuses on cross border care with approved hospitals and doctors, human review and travel coordination. Wellness travel focuses on destination flexible, curated retreats and programmes such as HAYAT-100, AAfiya and FFounders.

Important: nothing is confirmed until it has been verified. A particular hospital, treatment, provider, flight, visa or booking is never promised or confirmed by Hayat. A human coordinator verifies and confirms. Registering interest or sharing details is free and is not a booking or payment commitment.

What happens after a visitor registers. The visitor confirms their details, the enquiry is shared with the Trawellcare coordination team with their permission, and a coordinator reviews the conversation and reaches out to discuss next steps.
